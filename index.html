The way I look at brand building

Today, brand building is no longer confined to the traditional boundaries of B2B and B2C marketing. A strong brand can be built around its own identity, storytelling and the specific audience it wants to reach.

For Hunar, I'd focus on creating a distinct brand narrative tailored to the businesses, decision-makers and industries we're targeting. Strong branding creates familiarity, credibility and organic awareness over time, reducing our dependence on continuously spending on paid platforms like Meta just to reach potential customers. As this awareness compounds, it creates a lasting brand advantage that paid distribution alone cannot replicate.

Where I see
the opportunity

01.
Where I see the opportunity

The product has a story. The brand needs to communicate it.

After exploring Hunar's website and its presence across Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn, I see an opportunity to bring greater consistency and direction to its brand communication.

The platforms are active and content is being published, but the overall narrative and presentation could be much more cohesive. The content needs a recognisable identity, with every platform contributing to the same larger brand story.

YouTube, in particular, could benefit from stronger production quality across scripting, audio, editing and presentation. For a company building sophisticated AI technology, the quality of communication should reflect the quality of the product.

When content doesn't convey the same level of professionalism and innovation, it creates a gap between what the company offers and how potential customers perceive it.

My first priority would be to establish a consistent brand identity and bring the quality of communication in line with the value of the product.

LinkedIn

04.
LinkedIn

Turn the people behind Hunar into brand advocates.

LinkedIn should extend beyond the company page. The founders, leadership team, employees and customer-facing teams can collectively help build Hunar's visibility and credibility.

Each person can contribute through their own perspective and expertise.

  • Founders sharing company vision, industry insights and product thinking.
  • Product and engineering teams sharing technology stories and innovations.
  • Sales and customer-facing teams sharing industry observations and customer challenges.
  • Employees sharing their experiences and perspectives on the future of work.

Rather than publishing identical posts across every account, each person's content should have an authentic voice while contributing to a consistent brand narrative.

The idea is to expand Hunar's reach through people, not just its company page.
